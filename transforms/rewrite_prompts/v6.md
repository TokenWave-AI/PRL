You review a trajectory of an AI agent that solved a task in a Linux terminal: it passed the task's tests. Remove the agent's mistakes from it, mostly by deleting them, so that what remains is the agent's own correct work in its own words. The result will be used to train the agent: every word you add is a word the agent did not write, so add almost nothing.

## What to remove
Go through the trajectory step by step. Remove only what is clearly wrong.

1. Wrong statements in the reasoning. A sentence that contradicts the task or what the terminal showed (a misread output, a wrong count, a wrong belief about what a command does): delete that sentence.
2. Failed or useless turns. A turn whose command failed or did nothing the solution needed, when no later step relies on what it did or showed: delete the turn. Keep a turn whose output a later step uses, even if it was a detour.
3. Only when deleting a wrong sentence would leave the reasoning contradictory or unreadable, replace it with the shortest correct sentence instead (a few words, the agent's own style). Never add reasoning, checks or explanations the agent did not have.

## What to leave exactly as it is
4. Everything that is not wrong: hesitation ("maybe", "I think"), self-corrections ("wait", "actually"), exploration, alternatives the agent considered, checks of its own work, even when they look redundant. Do not shorten or polish.
5. All commands. Do not rewrite a command; if a wrong command was followed by a fix, delete the wrong turn only if nothing later depends on it.
6. The order of the turns: do not add, merge or reorder turns.
7. Steps {protected}: they complete the task. Do not delete them; you may delete a wrong sentence in their reasoning.

## Keep it consistent
8. No hindsight: what remains may only use the task and what the terminal had shown before each step.
9. After deleting a turn or sentence, check every later step: reasoning that refers to something no longer there. Delete that reference too; if a later terminal output would now be different, do not delete the turn.

## Decide
10. Remove as little as possible: at most {budget} of the {n} steps deleted, and all edits together may change at most {change_share} of the agent's text. Most successful trajectories have nothing clearly wrong: then answer with no edits.
11. Decide whether the edited trajectory would still pass the task's tests. If it would not, answer with no edits.

## How to write the edits
- edit_thinking: "old" is a short exact quote from that step's reasoning, copied character for character (one sentence or less), that occurs exactly once there; "new" is empty to delete it, or the shortest correct replacement.
- delete: removes the step's whole turn and the terminal output after it.
