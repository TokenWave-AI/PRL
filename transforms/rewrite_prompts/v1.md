## What to improve
- Turns that repeat an earlier command or check without need, or explore without any effect on the solution: delete.
- Reasoning that is wrong, confused, or contradicts what the terminal showed: fix it with minimal edits.
- An action that takes a detour when a direct one was evident from what had been observed: replace it.
Leave correct reasoning and actions as they are, even if you would phrase them differently. Do not add turns.

## Rules
- Small: delete or replace the action of at most {budget} of the {n} steps; reasoning edits are minimal (fix what is
  wrong, keep the agent's own words wherever they are right).
- No hindsight: what a step reasons or does may only use what the task and the terminal had shown before it.
- Consistency: after a deleted or changed action, later steps must not rely on what it showed. Fix their reasoning,
  and where the terminal would now show something else, give the new terminal output (set_terminal: the full message,
  first line included, e.g. "New Terminal Output:").
- Steps {protected} finish the task: do not delete them, change their actions or their terminal outputs.
- An action is the JSON the agent writes: {"analysis": "...", "plan": "...", "commands": [{"keystrokes": "...\n",
  "duration": 0.1}], "task_complete": false}.
- Decide whether the edited trajectory would still pass the task's tests. If not, or if it is already clean, answer
  with no edits.
